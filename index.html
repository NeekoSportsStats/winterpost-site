Run the post through Frostfall

Winterpost is a game for iPhone about a kennel of sled dogs in the snowbound region of Frostfall. Your dogs haul deliveries between towns for coins and race the trails against rival teams for medals and trophies. Buy towns, build sleds, train your dogs and adopt new breeds at the Lodge as they unlock.

  • Thirteen dog breeds, each with its own strengths on ice, powder and forest trail.
  • A hand-painted map of eight towns and the trails between them.
  • Races that turn on pace, wind, paws, focus and heart.
  • Tundra, an old lead dog, shows you the ropes.
  • No account, no ads, and no data collected. Your kennel is saved on your phone.

If a purchase didn't arrive, open the game again first: an unfinished purchase is delivered the next time the game starts. The Starter Pack, a season pass and a Kennel club membership bought before can be brought back with Settings > Restore purchases.

To cancel a Kennel club membership, use your phone's subscription settings - see the terms.
