Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

In short: Winterpost has no accounts and collects no personal data. Your kennel is saved on your own device. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play, and we never see your payment details. On the App Store the game's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Who we are

Winterpost is made by Neeko Sports Stats, a registered business name in Australia ("we", "us"). You can reach us at:

matthew@neekostats.com.au

What we collect

Nothing. Winterpost does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and there is nothing to sign in to. The game does not use advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking of any kind, and it does not send your game data to us or to anyone else.

Your saved game

Your kennel - your dogs, towns, coins, gems and progress - is saved in the app's own storage on your device, and so are your settings, what your dogs wear, your season pass progress and your Kennel club membership's renewal date. It stays there. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to any server.

Like any app's data, it may be included in your device's backups (iCloud, Google, or a computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are kept by Apple, Google or on your own computer under your settings, not by us.

Settings in the game can copy your save as text, so you can move it to another device yourself. That text only goes where you paste it.

Purchases

In-app purchases - gem packs, the Starter Pack, the season pass and the Kennel club membership - are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, Google account, card or other payment details. When a purchase completes, the store gives the app a record of which item was bought, a transaction number and, for the Kennel club, when the membership runs to. The game keeps these on your device only, so that each purchase is delivered exactly once and a membership is honoured until it ends.

The stores' handling of your purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

Winterpost does not send push notifications and has no server to send them from. With your permission, it schedules local notifications on your device: when a delivery reaches its town, when a dog's training finishes, and when your race tickets are full again. They are made and shown by your device alone and nothing is sent anywhere. The game asks once, after its tutorial; you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device's settings.

Software we use

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's in-app purchasing library to talk to the stores. The game does not switch on Unity's analytics, advertising, crash reporting or online services.

Children

Winterpost collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time: delete the app, or use Settings > Reset progress in the game. See Deleting your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the game ever starts collecting any information, this page will say exactly what, before the version that does so is released.
