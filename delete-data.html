Deleting your data

Last updated 18 October 2026

Winterpost has no accounts and keeps your game only on your device, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server - you are in control of all of it.

Start again, keeping the app

  1. Open Winterpost and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Reset progress at the bottom.
  3. Tap Continue, type reset and tap Reset.

Your saved game is erased and the game starts fresh. This cannot be undone.

Delete everything

Delete the app. On iPhone, touch and hold the Winterpost icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Everything the game stored on your device is removed with it.

If your device is backed up to iCloud, Google or a computer, older backups may still contain the app's data until those backups are replaced or deleted. On iPhone, iCloud backups are in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Backups.

Purchases

Resetting or deleting the game does not refund purchases, and does not cancel a Kennel club membership - see how to cancel. The Starter Pack, a season pass and a Kennel club membership can be restored with Settings > Restore purchases in the game. Gem packs are used up when bought and cannot be restored. Your purchase history is kept by Apple or Google, not by us.
